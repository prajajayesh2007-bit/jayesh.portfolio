About Me

Bridging low-level circuit dynamics with high-level software engineering.

The ECS Intersection

I am a second-year B.Tech Electronics & Computer Science (ECS) student at Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering (SLRTCE). My passion lies in understanding how computing works from the ground up: from individual transistors and logic gates to firmware, operating principles, and web interfaces.

Rather than staying confined to either purely hardware or purely abstract code, I thrive at their intersection. Whether it's wiring an ESP32 microcontroller with environmental sensors or writing clean, efficient C++ and Python algorithms to process live telemetry data, I love building end-to-end solutions.
